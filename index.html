About

The infrastructure work nobody sees — until it breaks.

I got into marketing through the back door: fixing a client's CRM that was quietly losing leads between form fill and follow-up. That gap — between what a marketing team thinks is happening and what their tools are actually doing — is where I've spent most of my career.

Today I work with founders, marketing leads, and agencies who've outgrown manual follow-up and spreadsheet attribution. I map how leads actually move through their stack, then rebuild the CRM, email, ad, and outbound systems so that growth doesn't depend on someone remembering to send a reminder.

I work fully remote, across time zones, and I keep a small number of active engagements at a time so nothing I build gets left half-wired.
